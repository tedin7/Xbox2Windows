# Compute SDWA packing and per-lane LDS slots

The synthetic SDWA fixture writes packed half results to a raw UAV. It covers
rounding toward zero, a negative half subnormal, finite overflow, byte and word
source selection, source sign extension and a preserved destination half.
The LDS fixture uses 64 threads. Each writes two qwords to a 16-byte slot and
reads both qwords from the lane at local X XOR 32 after a group barrier.

The WARP runner compares both fixtures against CPU-generated words and checks
the untouched guard region. Three controls must produce a readback mismatch.
They substitute nearest rounding, zero extension or a same-lane read.

```powershell
ctest --preset dev --no-tests=error -V -R '^startup_compute_lds_b64_(controls|warp|(slot-same-lane|rtz-nearest|rtz-zero-extend)_warp)$'
```

The checks passed. The last command ran 158 tests with 19 skips. The assembler
check reproduced four fixture binaries and five instruction snippets. The
fixture sets the packed RTZ source sign-extension bit separately because the
assembler rejects the textual modifier for this opcode. The two new fixtures
hit the original refusals at the base revision. The two original LDS fixtures
generated byte-identical HLSL before and after the change. The Windows dispatch
and its readback controls await execution on the test machine.

The tested instruction and addressing forms are described in the [RDNA 2 ISA reference](https://docs.amd.com/v/u/en-US/rdna2-shader-instruction-set-architecture).
