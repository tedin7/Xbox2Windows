# Startup translator: binary16 VOPC compares

`tools/startup/gfx2hlsl.py` lowers the binary16 VOPC compares `v_cmp_<relation>_f16` (`_e32` into VCC, `_e64` into an SGPR pair or `vcc_lo`) for the fifteen relations of the ISA except `tru` (#1723). Each source is the low half of a VGPR, SGPR or an inline binary16 constant, converted exactly with `f16tof32`; the relation of the converted values is the relation of the halves. The ordered relations are false with a NaN, the negated ones true, `lg` is less-or-greater, and `o` and `u` test the NaN on the half's bits (`|bits| > 0x7c00`). The `eq`, `le`, `ge` and `neq` forms read the same NaN test, so that a compare of a register with itself is not folded to a constant by DXC. Still refused: the SDWA forms, `v_cmp_ne_f16`, `tru`, and a lane mask (`exec_lo`, `vcc_lo`, a tracked mask SGPR) as a source, because the translator keeps one lane bit and not the 16-bit word the hardware would read. Reproduce: `ctest --preset dev --no-tests=error -R '^startup_gfx2hlsl_f16_cmp_draw_' -V` (translation controls, then FXC 5_1 and DXC 6_0 draws on WARP or hardware against the IEEE relation of the binary32 images, with negative controls, including same-source NaN pairs) and `python -B tests/startup_gfx2hlsl_mask_data_check.py` for the lane-mask refusals.
